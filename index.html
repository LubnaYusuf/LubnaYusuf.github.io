AboutLubna Yusuf · THINK STUDIOS

Lubna Yusuf

Author, filmmaker and lawyer, Lubna Yusuf works across creative forms — from poetry and films to photographs, paintings, illustrations and stories.

Her photographic practice is an extension of that curiosity: looking closely at people, places, atmosphere and the quiet details that often disappear when we stop paying attention.
